About the club

Curiosity meets practice.

The Algorithmic Trading Club brings together students interested in finance, markets, and technology. We make complex ideas approachable and give members room to learn by asking questions and building together.

Whether you are exploring your first market concepts or already working with data and code, you can find a place to contribute and grow.

What we do

Learn it. Try it. Share it.

Activities connect market ideas with practical technical skills.

01 · SESSIONS

Sessions

Explore market basics, trading concepts, and the tools behind them in welcoming peer-led discussions.

02 · WORKSHOPS

Workshops

Practise with data, Python, strategy ideas, and risk concepts through guided, hands-on activities.

03 · COMPETITIONS

Competitions

Put your thinking to work with team challenges that reward clear reasoning and thoughtful collaboration.

04 · RESEARCH

Research

Investigate questions about markets and algorithms, then share what you learn with the club.

FAQ

A few quick answers.

Do I need trading experience?

No. Activities are designed to welcome beginners as well as students with experience.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You can explore business and market topics first, and learn technical skills at your own pace.

Who can join?

Business, IT, engineering, and master's students with an interest in finance or technology are welcome.

What happens after I take the quiz?

It gives you a starting point and helps you discover topics to explore with the club.
